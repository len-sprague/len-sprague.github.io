Undergraduate honors thesis (Item 141). Uses quantitative <sup>1</sup>H NMR with an internal maleic acid standard, plus 2D NMR, to track the transesterification of glycerol fatty acid triesters in biodiesel production over time — work aimed at informing future catalyst design.
