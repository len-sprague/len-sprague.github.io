PLeW is an interactive tool for exploring complex data landscapes and human evaluation data, co-developed as part of ongoing collaborative research (see related work: *PLeW-NLG: Interactive Exploration of Human Evaluation Data across NLG Tasks*, INLG 2026, and *Collaborative Exploration of Complex Data Landscapes with PleW*, Oral Archives Journal).
