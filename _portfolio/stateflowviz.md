State Flow Mapper visualizes how a categorical field changes over time, built to compare change logs from one or more raters/annotators side by side across arc, circular, force-directed, timeline, and Sankey pass-flow layouts. Drop in your own CSV files to explore your data, or browse the built-in [examples](https://len-sprague.github.io/stateflowviz/examples/).
